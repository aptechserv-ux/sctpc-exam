Please read carefully

  1. The examination contains 30 multiple-choice questions — 10 each from Applications, Office Tools, and Technology.
  2. Each question has exactly one correct answer among four options (A, B, C, D).
  3. A countdown timer will be visible at all times. The exam will auto-submit automatically when time expires.
  4. Use the Question Palette to jump to any question, and to see which questions are answered, unanswered, or flagged for review.
  5. You may flag a question and revisit it later before final submission.
  6. Do not refresh the page or close the browser tab — your progress is saved automatically, but exiting the exam window may be treated as a violation.
  7. Once you click Submit Exam (or the timer runs out), your answers are final and cannot be changed.
  8. Each candidate is permitted only one attempt. Re-login after submission is not allowed.
